# Privacy Policy — VaultTabs

**Effective Date:** November 21, 2026  
**Publisher:** ARIS PARTY LLC

## Zero Telemetry & Local-First Design
VaultTabs runs locally inside your Chrome browser. We do **not** collect, sell, rent, or transmit your browsing history, page contents, keystrokes, or personal analytics to any third-party tracking service.

## Data Stored Locally
All extension preferences, rules, and user-created items are stored exclusively in Chrome's local/sync extension storage (`chrome.storage`) on your device.

## Optional Pro Upgrades (Stripe)
If you choose to upgrade to **VaultTabs Pro**, checkout is processed securely by **Stripe** (`buy.stripe.com`). We never see or store your payment card details. When you restore a Pro purchase, only your checkout email address and product identifier are verified against Stripe to confirm entitlement.

## Contact
For privacy or billing support, contact ARIS PARTY LLC via the Chrome Web Store support link or open an issue on this repository.
